## Learner-facing example: role assignment

**Competency:** Roles & Responsibilities  
**Where am I going?** The team should explicitly establish and confirm responsibilities, using the existing SME-defined standard.  
**How am I going?** In this example, the consultants flagged absent role assignment; supporting transcript span must be linked. This is a team observation, not proof of one learner's failure.  
**Reflection:** How were responsibilities established? What did I personally say or confirm?  
**Where to next?** At the start of the next scenario, practise explicit role allocation and acknowledgement.

## How the views differ

| | Learner | Instructor |
|---|---|---|
| Primary unit | Own actions/responsibilities; team interactions as context | Whole team and competency pattern |
| Main question | What should I understand and do next? | What should we discuss and practise? |
| Structure | Goal -> evidence -> reflection -> action | Group overview -> evidence -> debrief -> group goal |
| Theory | Hattie feed up/back/forward; Nicol self-assessment and closing gap | Nicol dialogue and assessment information for teaching |
| Risk | False individual attribution | Over-aggregation and unsupported priority |

## Design constraints

All seven categories remain visible; do not rank them until priority criteria are justified. Include both positive and negative behaviour examples when evidence exists. Keep transcript evidence and any uncertainty accessible. Never invent a score, clinical consequence, or precise timestamp.
