## Evidence chain

- §3.3, Figure 5: the existing system already produces utterance-linked, category/polarity-labelled participant feedback.
- §4.2: 24 participants, eight teams, four per condition. Table 5 reports Summarisation & Re-evaluation changes of −0.167 (automated) and +0.321 (human), averaged across evaluators.
- §§4.2–5: small preliminary study; static worksheet and interactive verbal debrief differ in content and delivery. Neither non-inferiority/equivalence nor causal failure of worksheet format is established.
- Hattie & Timperley (2007) and Nicol & Macfarlane-Dick (2006) support the design rationale, not local effectiveness or a mandatory layout.
- v0.1 tests support extraction feasibility and expose incomplete coverage, generic actions and attribution uncertainty. They do not establish target-user need or educational benefit.
