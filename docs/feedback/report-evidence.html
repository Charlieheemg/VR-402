Reflection
How did you establish who owned each task? What evidence showed that the intended receiver understood?
Next practice · feed forward
Practise naming the intended recipient, stating the task and seeking acknowledgement. The educator should confirm the wording and applicability to the scenario.
Interpretation boundary

3. Proposed evaluation framework and current progress

The design pedagogies specify feedback content; evaluation requires evidence for its interpretation and use. Cook and Hatala argue that validity concerns the intended interpretation and decision in a particular context. [23] Carless and Boud distinguish understanding feedback from taking action on it. [22] Accordingly, evaluation separates feedback fidelity and quality, immediate educational usefulness, and subsequent learning effectiveness. This is a project-specific synthesis, not a validated three-level instrument.

Research questions and operational definitions

RQ-B1 — Feedback fidelity and quality: Can learner- and instructor-facing feedback preserve assessment evidence, meaning, category/polarity and defensible individual/team attribution? How clear, relevant and actionable is the output when assessed separately? RQ-B2 — Immediate educational usefulness: Compared with generic feedback from identical evidence, does theory-guided feedback improve learner comprehension, corrective learning-plan quality and instructors’ preparation of accurate, actionable debriefs? RQ-B3 — Subsequent learning effectiveness: Does theory-guided feedback improve communication performance in subsequent simulation scenarios? All three questions remain unanswered. The original assessments and expert annotations belong to the upstream research team.

Comprehension concerns interpretation of expected and observed behaviour, including performance-gap recognition and evidence limitations. Corrective learning-plan quality concerns a feasible, specific and appropriate proposed response. Debrief preparation quality concerns accurate, evidence-supported discussion themes and actionable facilitation plans. Each requires prespecified anchors and rater guidance; none is an established score for this interface.

3.1 Feedback fidelity and quality

Fidelity is a prerequisite: reviewers will check source traceability, rubric category/polarity, contextual meaning, supported speaker/role/team attribution, omitted repairs and unsupported additions. Clarity, recipient relevance and actionability will be assessed separately, without combining them with fidelity into an unvalidated total score. Clinical experts may review appropriateness; target users may judge comprehensibility.

The Feedback Quality Instrument provides candidate review domains but was developed for educator–learner conversations, not static AI reports. [24] A 2024 scoping review identified limited evidence for digital feedback assessment tools. [26] Any adapted items therefore require local content review and piloting. The upstream team’s expert annotations provide assessment evidence, not validation of this presentation.

3.2 Immediate educational usefulness

Learner outcomes are comprehension of expected and observed behaviour—including recognition of performance gaps—and the quality of corrective learning plans. Instructor outcomes are the accuracy, usefulness and quality of debrief preparation; review/preparation time is secondary. A stated intention or plan does not demonstrate actual feedback uptake, which requires observation of subsequent use. [22]

The tentative comparison uses a generic LLM summary and theory-guided feedback generated from the same fixed assessment evidence, with the worksheet as an optional baseline. Future protocol elements include counterbalanced cases, documented generation procedures, prespecified scoring anchors, blinded outcome ratings where feasible and a pilot-based sample-size justification. Learner and instructor outcomes will be analysed separately, accounting for repeated participants, cases and teams.

3.3 Subsequent learning effectiveness

Later communication performance will, where feasible, be independently assessed in comparable simulations using the existing seven competencies. Kirkpatrick distinguishes reactions, learning, workplace behaviour and downstream results; its use in healthcare simulation does not make these outcomes interchangeable. [25] [27] Improved comprehension or subsequent VR performance alone cannot establish retention, workplace transfer or patient benefit.

The upstream manuscript’s balance rate, B = (G − R)/(G + R), is a candidate metric only. It is undefined when G + R = 0 and depends on event definitions, annotation practices, team dependence and comparable opportunities to demonstrate behaviour. These assumptions require review before the metric is adopted.

The tentative procedures below require feasibility assessment before study registration.

Tentative feasibility and pilot procedures

Select 2–3 permitted episodes across at least two competencies, including positive and negative observations. Freeze source spans and rubric versions; retain original evidence and worksheets. Generate comparison outputs from identical inputs using documented prompts, model/version and settings where applicable.

An independent reviewer should log each claim, source pointer, category/polarity, attribution, omitted repairs, unsupported additions, decision and revision. Record pass, fail or unresolved with denominators; unavailable evidence remains unresolved. Separately review clarity, relevance and actionability with clinical experts and intended users as appropriate. Do not average these judgements with fidelity checks.

In a later approved pilot, counterbalance matched cases to avoid repeated exposure to versions of the same episode, prespecify learner and instructor scoring anchors, blind outcome raters where feasible and document disagreement. Use pilot feasibility and variance to justify sample size and repeated-measure analysis. Perceived usefulness and preparation time complement, rather than replace, source-supported task performance. Observe subsequent use before claiming feedback uptake.
