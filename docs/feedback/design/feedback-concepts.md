# Learner and instructor feedback concepts

**Status:** information architecture and synthetic illustration only. Not a completed prototype, deployed system, clinician-validated feedback or user-tested design. Do not substitute this example for an actual consultant annotation.

## Synthetic example: role confirmation

*Invented training dialogue for explaining the proposed format; not extracted from a real recording.*

- Team leader: “Alex, please prepare the airway equipment.”
- Team member: “Understood, I will prepare it.”
- Team leader: “Thank you; confirm when it is ready.”

This illustration does **not** establish whether the real project's rubric would mark the event positive or negative. A rubric-aligned interpretation requires the full context and SME criteria.

## Learner-facing candidate

**Behavioural target:** communicate and confirm task responsibilities (subject to approved rubric wording).  
**Observed evidence:** link to the original approved utterances and timestamps, not a paraphrase alone.  
**What the evidence does / does not show:** explain only the observed exchange; note if subsequent completion or non-verbal acknowledgement is unavailable.  
**Reflect:** “What did I explicitly acknowledge, and how did the team know the task was complete?”  
**Possible next practice:** rehearse a clearly stated acknowledgement and completion confirmation in a later scenario. This is a proposed action, not a measured improvement.

## Instructor-facing candidate

**Team topic:** clarity of task assignment and confirmation.  
**Evidence:** same source-linked episode, preserving all speakers and sequence.  
**Discussion question:** “How did the team establish who owned the task and whether it had been completed?”  
**Possible group practice:** rehearse the assignment–acknowledgement–confirmation sequence.  
**Caution:** do not infer a specific learner's failure from an incomplete team record.

## Design choices to resolve

- Summary-first cards versus transcript-first navigation.
- Whether an audio-replay link is permitted and available.
- How to surface missing/ambiguous evidence.
- How to retain positive observations and all seven rubric categories.
- How an instructor can correct or dispute a system-generated interpretation.

No clinical-severity score, individual competence rating or fixed priority ranking is proposed as validated.
