| Source | Publication type and scope | Finding or construct | Design implication and limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hattie & Timperley (2007) · [SAGE](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/003465430298487) | Conceptual synthesis | Goals, observed performance and next action organise feedback. | Link criterion, observation and practice. Local report effectiveness requires study. |
| Nicol & Macfarlane-Dick (2006 issue) · [Taylor & Francis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/03075070600572090) | Conceptual synthesis | Learners actively assess and use feedback. | Provide an episode-specific reflection task. Learner uptake requires evidence. |

| [29] Duff · [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38240623/) | Systematic review, 70 studies | Comparative evidence for many debriefing methods was limited. | Pilot facilitated discussion prompts and assess their quality in context. |

Full-text findings and review counts were not independently checked. |
| [31] Kıyak · [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41939019/) | Scoping review, 42 studies | Eight randomised trials, 22 studies without student data, and none assessing behaviour or patient outcomes. Accuracy varied. | Evaluate fidelity separately from usefulness. The review concerns LLM feedback, whereas v0.1 was tested in deterministic mode. |
| [32] Alnazawi · [Source](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s41077-026-00447-6) | Scoping review, 7 studies | AI mainly supplemented human debriefing. Evidence concentrated on feasibility, usability, accuracy and perceived value. | Keep educators in control of debrief decisions and test objective outcomes before claiming a benefit. |
