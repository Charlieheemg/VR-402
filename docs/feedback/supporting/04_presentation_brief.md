# Interim presentation outline

## Research narrative

The contribution concerns the educational use of existing communication assessments in paediatric VR simulation. It specifies a theory-guided transformation into learner and instructor feedback, with source traceability and defensible attribution. The evaluation will test fidelity before examining comprehension, corrective planning and subsequent communication performance.

## Methodological discussion points

- The contribution is a reproducible transformation method whose educational value requires comparison with generic summarisation.
- Post-simulation delivery supports complete-episode review and facilitated discussion; timing effects remain task-dependent.
- Learner and instructor views use the same evidence for different educational decisions.
- Assessment validity, recipient attribution and teaching priority are distinct decisions. No validated severity-weighting model is available.
- Current outputs are content prototypes. Source-fidelity and user-study results will be reported after the proposed evaluations are conducted.
