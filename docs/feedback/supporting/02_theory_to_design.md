# Theory-to-design construct matrix

**Research status:** educational-theory-derived design hypotheses, not validated effects of the proposed interface.

| Construct | Source basis | Operational meaning in this FYP | Proposed UI / content | Candidate test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Feed up / goal | Hattie & Timperley 2007 | Explicit target communication behaviour from SME rubric | Expected-behaviour line | Learner identifies expected standard |
| Feed back / discrepancy | Hattie & Timperley 2007 | Observed behaviour compared with target | Evidence quote + gap explanation | Accurate gap identification |
| Feed forward | Hattie & Timperley 2007 | Concrete next practice action | Suggested alternative + next goal | Expert-rated action-plan quality |
| Task/process feedback | Hattie & Timperley 2007 | Specific behaviour and strategy, not personal judgment | Describe observable episode and process repair | Evidence accuracy / actionability |
| Self-assessment | Nicol & Macfarlane-Dick 2006 | Learner evaluates own episode against standard | Reflection prompt | Quality of self-evaluation |
| Opportunity to close gap | Nicol & Macfarlane-Dick 2006 | Rehearse target in next scenario | Practice target and optional S2 follow-up | S1-to-S2 behavioural change |
| Feedback dialogue | Nicol & Macfarlane-Dick 2006 | Learner/educator discuss interpretation | Instructor debrief question | Instructor evaluation |
| Information for teaching | Nicol & Macfarlane-Dick 2006, principle 7 | Teacher sees patterns requiring facilitation | Team overview, evidence drill-down | Debrief preparation and accuracy |
| Evidence traceability | Existing structured discourse manuscript | Link assessment to utterance IDs/span | Evidence source and role | Source-matching audit |

## Distinguish the decisions

1. **Validity gate:** only present an assessment with support in the existing rubric and transcript evidence; flag uncertain interpretation. This is an assessment-quality prerequisite, not a priority score.
2. **Audience routing:** individual, role, team or instructor relevance. Do not infer an individual's failure from a team-level observation without a defensible attribution rule.
3. **Emphasis / priority:** which observations deserve early discussion. **Not yet validated.** Do not assert that clinical severity, frequency, actionability, etc. form an established weighted model.

## Competing design options

- Raw worksheet: complete detail and source context, but reading burden and action selection may fall to recipient.
- Generic LLM summary: low development overhead, potentially personalised, but accuracy, evidence linkage and consistency require testing.
- Theory-guided structured feedback: explicit goals, evidence, self-assessment and next action, potentially reproducible; extra design and validation cost, not yet proven superior.

**Caution:** the theory does not prescribe dashboard layout, a specific number of highlighted issues, or severity weights. These remain hypotheses and choices for later testing.
