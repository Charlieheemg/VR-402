Source: **CDE4301 assessment brief and rubrics - AY2026 - interim assessment**, six pages, supplied in project files. This is a planning map, not a substitute for the original rubric.

| Criterion | Examiner/supervisor question | Current evidence | Missing evidence / action |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Objective, Scope and Context | What problem, contribution, and difference from prior work? | Existing manuscript, downstream feedback-design gap, precise scope | Show comparison to generic ChatGPT/worksheet and limitations of claimed novelty |
| A2 Methodology / concept development | Why suitable, compared with alternatives? | Two complementary foundational pedagogies, theory-to-design map | Explicit comparison with raw Excel, generic LLM summary and other debriefing approaches |
| A3 Fabrication of prototypes | What has been created and why? | Draft learner and instructor feedback mockups | Provide 2-3 source-linked annotated examples, versioned and visibly marked conceptual |

| A5 Analysis | What found, and how valid? | Existing study findings, annotation feasibility limitations | Separate published/team results from own findings; avoid raw-cell counts as performance metrics |
| A6 Problems and project plan | What shortcomings and viable milestones? | Unvalidated priority rules, individual attribution, access constraints | Detailed milestones, expert consultation plan, contingencies |
| B1 Presentation materials | Clear, accurate, well-annotated visuals | Planned pipeline + mockup comparison | Add citations, readable evidence labels, explicit ownership |
| B2 Delivery | Logical and concise | Suggested slide narrative | Rehearse within 30 minutes for group project |
| B3 Questions | Thoughtful, accurate answers | FAQ in presentation brief | Practise validity, novelty, limitations, and evaluation answers |
| C1-C2 Initiative | Motivation and meeting contribution | Weekly literature review, research-team engagement | Document dates, individual contributions and decisions |
