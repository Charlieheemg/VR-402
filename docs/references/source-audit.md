# Literature and source audit — checked 19 October 2026

Claims in the main report link to these sources. Publisher/author abstracts and official corpus documentation were used where full text was blocked. No unverified quotations are reproduced. Sample sizes are stated only when verified. This is not a systematic review.

| Source | Verified scope / report use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Jiang & Pell (2017), *The sound of confidence and doubt*, Speech Communication 88, 106–126, [10.1016/j.specom.2017.01.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.specom.2017.01.011) | Publisher abstract: expressive-confidence and perceptual/acoustic study | Supports investigating perceived confidence, not correctness or competence |
| Sabu & Rao (2020), *Automatic Prediction of Confidence Level from Children's Oral Reading Recordings*, Interspeech 3141–3145, [10.21437/Interspeech.2020-2276](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2020/sabu20_interspeech.html) | Original archive abstract/paper and existing dataset audit | Reading labels, agreement-selected subset; our three-class task differs from paper's low-vs-rest result |
| Smiljanić & Bradlow (2009), *Speaking and Hearing Clearly: Talker and Listener Factors in Speaking Style Changes*, Language and Linguistics Compass 3(1), 236–264, [10.1111/j.1749-818X.2008.00112.x](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20046964/) | Review abstract verified; cited as a review, not original experiment | Explains interacting talker/listener/signal factors |
| Krause & Braida (2004), *Acoustic properties of naturally produced clear speech at normal speaking rates*, JASA 115(1), 362–378, [10.1121/1.1635842](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14759028/) | Original abstract: clear/normal-rate acoustics; slowing alone insufficient | Does not mean rate never matters |
| Lam, Tjaden & Wilding (2012), *Acoustics of clear speech: effect of instruction*, JSLHR 55(6), 1807–1821, [10.1044/1092-4388(2012/11-0154)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22411282/) | Twelve speakers, 18 sentences, habitual/clear/hearing-impaired/overenunciate instructions | Instruction effects differ; cannot pool every elicitation as a single style |
| Gutz, Maffei & Green (2023), *Feedback From Automatic Speech Recognition to Elicit Clear Speech in Healthy Speakers*, AJSLP 32(6), 2940–2959, [10.1044/2023_AJSLP-23-00030](https://pubs.asha.org/doi/10.1044/2023_AJSLP-23-00030) | 23 healthy speakers, ages 23–40; Habitual, Clear, DeepSpeech feedback; source reports SLP ratings/transcriptions | Edinburgh computer-directed acting is not their feedback intervention; transfer unvalidated |
| Bent, Baese-Berk, Ryherd & Perry (2022), *Intelligibility of medically related sentences in quiet, speech-shaped noise, and hospital noise*, JASA 151(5), 3496, [10.1121/10.0011394](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35649935/) | Abstract: 69 young adults, 23 per listening condition; recognition affected by noise and lexical familiarity | Does not validate our application or imply hospital noise uniquely worse than speech-shaped noise |
| ASHA, [Dysarthria in Adults](https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/clinical-topics/dysarthria-in-adults/) | Professional definitions: intelligibility, comprehensibility, efficiency | Terminology source, not evidence that healthy clinicians have dysarthria |
| Mayo (2013), [Acted clear speech corpus, 10.7488/ds/138](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/items/e0d78c30-c38d-41a5-947c-b03186a50a97) | Actual download, readme, licence and segmentation criteria inspected | One speaker; no listener scores identified; redistribution permission not established |
| Ward, Robinson, Paradis, Tucker & Shirley (2019), *R²SPIN: Re-Recording the Revised Speech Perception in Noise Test*, Interspeech 3133–3137, [10.21437/Interspeech.2019-1281](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2019/ward19b_interspeech.html) | Original paper §§2–4 and [BBC README](https://github.com/bbc/r2spin): two new RP speakers; 16 listeners; keyword test; original babble alignment matters | Our logistic refit is not the authors' GEE significance analysis |
| [SLUSI official lab description](https://sites.google.com/nau.edu/applied-ling-speech-lab-nau/projects/osim-project/slusi-corpus) | 66 speakers; transcription-based scores for subset plus separate listener ratings | Not downloaded or modelled in this task; excerpt-to-audio mapping and terms still need audit |
| [Clarity CPC1 official description](https://claritychallenge.org/docs/cpc1/cpc1_intro) | Hearing-aid processed audio, listener characteristics, measured recognition; closed/open-set tracks | Candidate design precedent, not a completed project experiment |
| Eyben et al. (2010), [openSMILE, 10.1145/1873951.1874246](https://doi.org/10.1145/1873951.1874246); Eyben et al. (2016), [GeMAPS, 10.1109/TAFFC.2015.2457417](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAFFC.2015.2457417) | Existing documented extractor basis; actual feature count tested locally | Standardized extraction does not validate a psychological/perceptual construct |
