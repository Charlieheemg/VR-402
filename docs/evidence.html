Research history and access boundaries

The history register distinguishes superseded plans and report revisions from the current synthesis. Historical measurements and prototype files are retained. Raw clinical recordings, participant workbooks, restricted corpora and model binaries are not distributed.

Some experiments require privately held inputs. Public aggregates support inspection of reported results; they do not make every original analysis reproducible from a fresh public checkout. Each method page identifies its inputs and limitations.
