Duration and RMS use the complete, untrimmed file. RMS dBFS is recording level, not calibrated sound pressure. Pitch uses finite, positive openSMILE eGeMAPSv02 F0 estimates (at least five frames); median is in Hz and range is the 80th–20th percentile difference in semitones. Pitch tracks have not been checked against reference F0. Waveforms share a full-scale amplitude axis and are stretched to each clip’s duration.
