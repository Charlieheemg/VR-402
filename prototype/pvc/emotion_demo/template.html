CREMA-D: expressive takes use medium intended intensity; neutral is unspecified. Neutral comparisons therefore do not hold intensity constant.

Duration and RMS use the complete, untrimmed file. RMS dBFS is recording level, not calibrated sound pressure. Pitch uses finite, positive openSMILE eGeMAPSv02 F0 estimates (at least five frames); median is in Hz and range is the 80th–20th percentile difference in semitones. Pitch tracks have not been checked against reference F0. Waveforms share a full-scale amplitude axis and are stretched to each clip’s duration.

Reveal dataset labels and provenance

These are the actors’ intended emotion labels from filenames. They are neither model predictions nor human listener judgements for these clips. Perceptual ratings are not loaded.

Identical text omits vocal delivery. Acoustic differences alone do not establish different literal meanings, better communication, emotion recognition accuracy, or validity for paediatric VR speech.

Transcript baseline: published dataset script, supplied identically to both clips. No ASR or transcript-only model is run. Acoustic summaries are precomputed from the actual recordings; no emotion classifier is applied. Original audio is embedded for offline playback.
